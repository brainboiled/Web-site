Origins privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Origins is an educational alchemy game for Android and iOS, published by BrainBoiled (brainboiled.com). This page explains what the game does with your data. In short: it collects none.

What stays on your phone

The game saves on your phone, and only there, your game (discovered elements, recipes found, elements placed in the lab), the language you chose and whether human times have been purchased. This information never leaves the device. It is erased when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If your phone's backup is turned on, the system (Android or iOS) may include it in your personal backup, which Google or Apple manage under their own rules.

Purchasing human times

The purchase goes through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled by Google or Apple: we receive neither your name, your address nor your payment details. The game only receives the information that the purchase was made, in order to unlock the content.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No data sent to the publisher or to third parties.

The game connects to no server of its own: the purchase goes through the store app.

Children

The game is meant for a general audience and collects data from no one, children included.

Your rights

Because the game sends us no personal data, we hold nothing about you. For any question, including under the GDPR, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
