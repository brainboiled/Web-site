MorseTree – Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

MorseTree is an Android app published by brainboiled (brainboiled.com). It translates, plays and teaches Morse code. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: it collects none.

Microphone

The “Listen” mode uses the microphone to recognise Morse code. The sound is analysed live in your phone's memory. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is only used while this mode is on screen and after you have allowed it; you can withdraw that permission at any time in the Android settings.

What stays on your phone

The app saves on your phone, and only there, your settings (speed, tone, board, colour, theme, language), your progress through the lessons and the fact that the full version has been purchased. This information never leaves the device. It is erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Purchasing the full version

The purchase is handled by Google Play. The app sees neither your payment method nor your identity: Google Play only tells it whether the full version has been purchased. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No data sent to the publisher or to third parties.

Internet access is used only for the Google Play in-app purchase.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13 and collects data from no one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact@brainboiled.com.
