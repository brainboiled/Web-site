Diafra privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Diafra is a photography learning app published by BrainBoiled ("we"). This page explains what data the app uses and what it does with it.

In short

Diafra has no account, shows no ads, uses no analytics and sends no personal data to our servers: we do not have any.

Data the app uses

Camera. The "Camera" tab shows your phone's camera image so it can overlay framing guides. The image is processed on your phone and is never transmitted.

Photos. A photo is saved only if you press the shutter button and then choose to keep it. It is then added to your phone's gallery. Diafra does not read your other photos and does not send them anywhere.

Settings and progress. Language, theme, the camera chosen in the simulator, completed lessons and completed exercises are stored on your phone. If your phone's backup is turned on, the system (Android or iOS) may include these settings in your personal backup, which Google or Apple manage under their own rules.

Purchase of the full version. The purchase goes through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled by Google or Apple: we receive neither your name, your address nor your payment details. The app only receives the information that the full version has been purchased, in order to unlock it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your advertising ID or any usage statistics.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share any data. The only third parties involved are Google and Apple, for distributing the app, for payment and for your phone's backup.

Children

Diafra is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because the app sends us no personal data, we hold nothing about you. You can erase the app's data at any time from your phone's settings, or by uninstalling it. For any question, including under the GDPR, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
