FlowDay privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

FlowDay is a visual day planner for Android and iOS, published by BrainBoiled (brainboiled.com). This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: without an account, everything stays on your phone. The optional Pro features linked to Google send your activities to your own Google account, at your request. BrainBoiled has no server and receives none of your data.

What stays on your phone

  • your first name or nickname;
  • your activities: name, emoji, times, duration, travel, recurrence, reminders;
  • your settings: theme, language, first day of the week, chosen alarm sound (Android);
  • the status of your Pro subscription.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. On Android, the home screen widget shows your current activity. If your phone's backup is turned on, the system (Android or iOS) may include this data in your personal backup, which Google or Apple manage under their own rules.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders and alarms are scheduled on the phone itself; they go through no server. On Android, FlowDay asks for permission to send notifications, schedule exact alarms, show the alarm full screen and, if you accept, to be exempted from battery optimisation so reminders arrive on time. Access to audio files is only used to choose the alarm sound. On iOS, FlowDay asks for permission to send notifications. These permissions can be withdrawn at any time in the phone's settings.

Pro features linked to Google (optional)

If you sign in with your Google account, FlowDay receives your name and email address from Google, to show them in the settings, and access to two services:

  • Google Drive ("drive.appdata" scope): a backup of your activities and settings is stored in a hidden folder of your Drive reserved for FlowDay. FlowDay cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar ("calendar" scope): FlowDay creates a calendar named "FlowDay" and copies your activities into it. If you share your schedule, the email addresses you enter are sent to Google to give those people read-only access to that calendar.

This data goes directly from your phone to Google, encrypted (HTTPS). BrainBoiled does not receive it. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's processing is governed by its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

ICS export

The export creates a calendar file (.ics) with your activities on your phone. You choose where to send it, using your phone's share feature.

Pro subscription

The subscription goes through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled by Google or Apple: BrainBoiled receives neither your name nor your payment details. The app only receives the subscription status, to unlock the Pro features.

What FlowDay does not do

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No location.
  • No BrainBoiled server: no data is sent to the publisher.
  • No selling or renting of data.

Retention and deletion

  • Data on the phone: kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Google Drive backup: Settings > Delete my data erases it. You can also delete FlowDay's hidden data in Google Drive (Settings > Manage apps).
  • "FlowDay" calendar: it stays in your Google Calendar until you delete it; shares can be removed in the app or in Google Calendar.
  • FlowDay's access to your Google account: remove it at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Age of users

FlowDay is meant for teenagers aged 13 and over and for adults. The Google features require a Google account, subject to Google's age requirements.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability and objection provided by the GDPR. Because BrainBoiled holds none of your data, most of them are exercised directly in the app or in your Google account. For any question, write to us; we answer within 30 days. You can also contact your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
