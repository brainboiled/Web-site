RingWise privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

RingWise is an app for Android and iOS published by BrainBoiled (brainboiled.com). It scores sport shooting targets and analyses your groups. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: everything stays on your phone, nothing is sent to us.

What the app stores, and where

On your phone, and only there:

  • your sessions: hits, scores, series, how you felt and your fatigue noted at the end of a session, notes;
  • your equipment: gun records (make, model, sight, shot counter, maintenance) and ammunition (lots, stock, price);
  • the target photos you choose to score, copied into the app's folder;
  • your settings (discipline, session format, language).

The publisher has no access to any of this information. It is erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If your phone's backup is turned on, the system (Android or iOS) may include it in your personal backup, which Google or Apple manage under their own rules.

Camera and photos

RingWise opens the phone's camera or photo picker only when you want to photograph or choose a target. It does not browse your library: it only receives the photo you chose. On iOS, access is managed in the phone's settings and can be withdrawn at any time.

Sharing and backup

Nothing is shared automatically. When you export a PDF score sheet or a backup file, you choose where it goes, using your phone's share feature. The backup file contains your sessions and equipment, not the photos.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No Internet access: no data sent to the publisher or to third parties.
  • No in-app purchase.

Children

RingWise is meant for sport shooters and collects data from no one, children included.

Your rights

Because the app sends us no personal data, we hold nothing about you. To delete your data, clear the app's data or uninstall it (make a backup first if you want to keep it). For any question, including under the GDPR, write to us.

Changes

If the app changes (sync, usage statistics…), this policy will be updated before the version concerned and published on this page with its date.
