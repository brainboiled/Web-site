MorseTree privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

MorseTree is an app for Android and iOS published by BrainBoiled (brainboiled.com). It translates, plays and teaches Morse code. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: it collects none.

Microphone

The "Listen" mode uses the microphone to recognise Morse code. The sound is analysed live in your phone's memory: it is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is only used while this mode is on screen and after you have allowed it; you can withdraw that permission at any time in the Android or iOS settings.

What stays on your phone

The app saves on your phone, and only there, your settings (speed, tone, board, colour, theme, language), your progress through the lessons and whether the full version has been purchased. This information never leaves the device. It is erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If your phone's backup is turned on, the system (Android or iOS) may include it in your personal backup, which Google or Apple manage under their own rules.

Purchasing the full version

The purchase goes through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled by Google or Apple: we receive neither your name, your address nor your payment details. The app only receives the information that the purchase was made, in order to unlock the full version.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No data sent to the publisher or to third parties.

The app connects to no server of its own: Internet access is only used by the store, for the purchase.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13 and collects data from no one, children included.

Your rights

Because the app sends us no personal data, we hold nothing about you. For any question, including under the GDPR, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
